# Participating and contributing

## Submit challenge work

Build in your own project repository. Open the official daily Discussion linked in the hackathon email and add a reply using the [daily submission template](templates/daily-submission.md). Include evidence relevant to that day's instructions. You can return to a missed day's thread to catch up, subject to the official deadlines.

Do not open a pull request to this hub containing your entire project. For the finale, follow the official final-submission instructions and use the [final submission template](templates/final-submission.md).

## Ask for help

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/qodo-hackathon/software-factory-hackathon/discussions) for general questions, or reply in the relevant challenge thread for challenge-specific questions. Include the challenge, what you expected, what happened, and a minimal, redacted example where useful. Do not include secrets or confidential material.

For eligibility questions, accessibility needs, conduct reports, or prize issues, reply to an official hackathon email, as described in the [event rules](https://hackathon.qodo.ai/rules/). Do not post private reports or delivery details publicly.

## Improve this hub

For documentation errors or broken links, open an issue or a focused pull request describing the correction. Keep contributions limited to public participant-facing material. Do not add unreleased challenges, solutions, private planning documents, participant contact lists, or unconfirmed partner claims.

Be respectful and follow the [official fair-play and event rules](https://hackathon.qodo.ai/rules/). Only share material you have the right to share, and disclose relevant assistance and third-party material in your submission.
