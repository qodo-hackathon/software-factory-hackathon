# Final submission template

Use this template with the official final-submission instructions. A repository link alone is not an entry. Follow the [official rules](https://hackathon.qodo.ai/rules/) and the final Discussion instructions.

```markdown
## Project
Project name:
Repository link:
Final commit or release:

## What my software factory does
Explain the project and the workflow from a ticket or idea to a reviewed pull request.

## Walkthrough
Link a demo or provide a clear written walkthrough. Explain where human judgment remains necessary.

## Completed challenges
Link your daily Discussion submissions and the relevant deliverables.

## Verification and Qodo's role
Show how Qodo reviews, project standards, tests, or workflow checks influenced your work and decisions. Link supporting evidence and explain remaining limitations.

## Reproduce the result
Setup instructions, prerequisites, and a safe example to try. Identify optional tools that may require accounts or incur costs. Never include credentials.

## My contribution and assistance
Explain what you built or improved during the hackathon. Disclose pre-existing work, AI assistance, other help, and third-party materials with appropriate licenses or attribution.

## Known limitations
Describe unfinished work, failure cases, and where the workflow requires human review.
```

Keep submitted links accessible throughout judging. Review all code, screenshots, logs, and links for confidential information, credentials, unauthorized personal data, and material you do not have permission to share before posting.
