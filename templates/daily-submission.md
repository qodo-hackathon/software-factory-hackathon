# Daily submission template

Copy the template below into a reply to the official Discussion for the challenge you completed. Replace the prompts with your own information and follow any additional instructions for that day. Do not open a separate submission thread unless the official instructions ask you to.

```markdown
## Challenge
Day/date and challenge title:

## Project
Project name:
Repository link:
Relevant pull request or commit:

## What I completed
Describe what you built or changed for this challenge.

## Evidence
Link the requested deliverables. Include relevant Qodo review findings, tests, standards, or workflow results where the challenge asks for them.
Explain what the evidence demonstrates; note any remaining limitations.

## What I learned / what is blocked
What worked, what changed after verification, and any unresolved issues.

## Assistance and third-party material
Identify relevant AI assistance, reused code/resources, collaborators' help, and applicable licenses or attribution.

## Optional demo
Screenshot, short video, or walkthrough link, if useful.
```

**Before posting:** check that links can be accessed as required for judging, remove secrets and unauthorized personal or confidential data, and ensure you have permission to share the material. Do not post registration emails or other private contact information.
